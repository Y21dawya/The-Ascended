The Ascended - an Endless Sky plugin
This plugin adds an alien race who have used their T4/T5 technology in order to hide themselves. I plan to make their space inaccessible to the player for a large portion of the game, but I still haven't worked out a way of explaining how you access their space. A minimum requirement to encounter this race would be the Heliarch expedition, and also the Free Worlds storyline (in order to make sure that the player has read the lore about hyperspace lanes having the potential to change (which the heliarch explains has happened near Yed Prior, where I would plan to create an access point to Ascended space.

## Things to do after forking:
1. Clone your repo locally. You'll want to clone / symlink your repo within the `"plugins"` directory in your "user" directory (which is where Endless Sky writes your pilot savegames). This ensures you can update your local version of Endless Sky without worrying about losing any uncommitted & unpushed data.
2. Flesh out your content! You can learn about all the different bits of game data in the [Endless Sky wiki](https://github.com/endless-sky/endless-sky/wiki/CreatingPlugins).
3. [Submit your plugin for distribution](https://github.com/endless-sky/endless-sky/wiki/PluginRepository).
