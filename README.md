The Ascended - an Endless Sky plugin

This plugin will add an alien race who have used their T4/T5 technology in order to hide themselves. I plan to make their space inaccessible to the player for a large portion of the game, but I still haven't worked out a way of explaining how you access their space. A minimum requirement to encounter this race would be the Heliarch expedition, and also the Free Worlds storyline (in order to make sure that the player has read the lore about hyperspace lanes having the potential to change (which the Heliarch expedition explains has happened near Yed Prior, where I would plan to create an access point to Ascended space. I am aware that this would refer to Sheragi space, but I think it could work)
